Piano Scales Lab

Scales, arpeggios, chords and a full tempo trainer for every key. Built for pianists who want to practise smarter.

What's in the app
  • 🎹

    Scales & Arpeggios

    Every scale type in all 12 keys with correct fingering for both hands. Includes chromatic, melodic minor and arpeggios.

    →
  • 📈

    Tempo Trainer

    Set a start and target BPM. The app increases tempo automatically. Scale Cycle rotates through multiple keys at increasing tempos.

    →
  • 🎵

    Chord Progressions

    Every chord in the current key on a scrollable keyboard. Loop progressions from a built-in library across Pop, Blues, Jazz and more.

    →
  • 🎸

    Pads

    Chord, drum and melody pads — all three running simultaneously. A performance playground built into your practice tool.

    →
  • ✏️

    Fingering Editor

    View and customise fingering for every scale. Built-in defaults, fully editable and saved between sessions.

    →
  • 🥁

    Metronome

    Full-featured metronome with tap tempo, custom time signatures and multiple click sounds.

    →
  • 🎛️

    Synth & Sound

    Eight instrument presets with full parameter control. Shape the sound to suit how you practise.

    →
  • 📖

    Music Theory Reference

    Scales, keys, chords and fingering numbers explained. A handy reference built into the app.

    →
  • ⚙️

    Themes & Settings

    Four themes, six colour palettes, Bluetooth delay compensation and fully configurable defaults.
