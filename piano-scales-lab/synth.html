Piano Scales Lab

Synth & Sound

Shape the sound of your keyboard and scale playback

The Tone panel controls the sound character of the piano keyboard and scale playback. Tap the wave icon in the top right corner of any page to open it.

Presets

  • Piano — classic acoustic piano tone with natural decay
  • E.Piano — electric piano with gentle warmth
  • Cosmic — spacious evolving pad with modulation
  • Synth — bright synthesiser lead tone
  • Organ — sustained organ with harmonics that hold
  • Flute — soft breathy flute-like tone
  • Sax — reedy saxophone character
  • Harpsi — harpsichord with sharp attack and quick decay

Parameters

  • Wave — morphs from warm triangle to bright sawtooth
  • Detune — chorus effect by layering a second detuned voice
  • Modulation — vibrato depth combined with reverb
  • Vibrato Rate — speed of the vibrato LFO in Hz
  • Attack — how quickly the note reaches full volume
  • Decay — how long the note rings after the initial peak
  • Sustain — 0 = piano-style, 1 = organ-style (holds indefinitely)
  • Filter Q — resonance; higher values add a wailing quality
  • Stereo Width — spreads sound across the stereo field

Test Note

The Test button plays a C4 note so you can hear your changes in real time without leaving the panel.

Restore Defaults

Resets all parameters back to the default Piano preset.

For a realistic piano sound, try the Piano preset then reduce Stereo Width slightly if you prefer a more centred sound.
