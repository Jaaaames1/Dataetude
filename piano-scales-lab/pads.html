Piano Scales Lab

Melody & Chord Pads

A performance playground with three interactive pad modes

The Pads page is the most interactive part of the app. Three modes — Chord, Drum and Melody — can all run simultaneously.

Switching Modes

Tap the mode selector button at the top to switch between Chord, Drum and Melody pads.

8 or 12 Pads

The pad count button toggles between 8 and 12 pads across all three modes. 12 pads extends the range with additional notes and drum sounds.

Chord Pads

  • Tap the key button to change key
  • Maj/Min toggles between major and minor chord qualities
  • Chord Pattern selects which progression plays in the background
  • ▶ starts and stops the chord loop
  • ↻ toggles looping — press while looping to play out the bar then stop
  • Arp switches to arpeggio mode
  • Key Up queues a key change at the next bar 1
  • Key Reset returns to the original key

Drum Pads

  • Simple, Disco, Rock, Breakdown — patterns switch cleanly on beat 1
  • Fill — triggers a drum fill on the next bar
  • ▶ starts and stops the drum loop
  • Move — rearrange pads into any order
  • 12 pads adds Rim, Cowbell, Shaker and Ride

Melody Pads

  • Pads are colour-coded by scale degree
  • Multiple fingers play multiple notes simultaneously
  • Maj/Min switches the scale quality at beat 1
  • Arp plays notes as arpeggios instead of sustained chords
  • Key Up queues a key change at beat 1

Layering All Three Together

Start a drum pattern, add a chord loop, then play melody on top. Drums snap to beat 1 automatically when added to a running loop.

Queue a new drum pattern while playing — it switches cleanly on the next bar 1, never mid-bar.
