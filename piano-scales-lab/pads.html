Looking for the pads?

Drum, chord and melody pads are not part of Piano Scales Lab. They live in their own app, Pads Loop Lab, which has drum beats, chord loops, melody pads and custom samples that you can record, layer and loop.

Piano Scales Lab focuses on scales, arpeggios, chords, the Tempo Trainer, the Fingering Editor and the metronome.
