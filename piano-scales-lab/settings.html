Piano Scales Lab

Themes, Palettes & Settings

Customise the app to suit how you practise

Open settings by tapping the gear icon in the top right corner of any page.

Themes

  • Dark — the default dark theme, easy on the eyes
  • Midnight — deeper and richer dark theme, true blacks
  • Cream — warm off-white light theme
  • Light — clean bright white theme

Key Highlight Palettes

Six colour palettes control how left and right hand scale notes are highlighted on the piano keyboards.

Haptic Feedback

Toggles vibration when you tap keys. Turn it off if you find it distracting.

Show Note Names Below Keys

Toggles the C D E F G A B labels displayed beneath every piano key across the whole app.

Dark Piano Keys

Switches the piano keyboard to a dark colour scheme. Purely visual — scale highlighting and fingering numbers are unaffected.

Bluetooth Delay

If you use Bluetooth headphones, there is typically a short delay between the keyboard visual flashing and the note sound reaching your ears. This setting shifts the keyboard flash to match.

CodecSuggested starting point
SBC (most common)150ms
AAC / Samsung100ms
aptX80ms
aptX Low Latency40ms

Not sure which codec your headphones use? Start at 150ms and reduce until the visuals feel in sync. Leave at 0ms for wired headphones or the speaker.

Keep Playing When Screen Locks

Keeps the metronome or drum loops running when the screen turns off.

Keep Screen On During Training

When enabled, the screen stays awake automatically during a Tempo Trainer session. Reverts to its previous state when the session ends.

Default Key

Sets which key the app opens in every time you launch it.

Default Scale Type

Sets which scale type is selected on launch.

Default Pad Mode

Sets whether the Pads page opens on Chord, Drum or Melody mode by default.

Default Pad Count

Sets whether the app opens with 8 or 12 pads.

Default Metro Sound

Sets which metronome click sound is used on launch. Your choice is saved automatically whenever you change the sound on the Metro page.

All defaults apply every time you launch the app — set them to match your usual practice routine and the app is ready to go the moment you open it.
