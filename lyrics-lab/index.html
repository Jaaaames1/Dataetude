What's in the app
  • ✍️

    Lyric Editor

    Section-based editor with Verse, Chorus, Bridge and more. Drag to resize, duplicate or reorder. Live word and line counts throughout.

    →
  • 👁️

    View Mode

    Clean read-only view of your lyrics with single and double column layouts, zoom and fullscreen. PDF export built in.

    →
  • 📚

    Song Library

    All your songs in one place with search, sort by date or title and timestamps. Auto-incrementing names so you can start writing immediately.

    →
  • 🔤

    Rhyme Finder

    Type any word and get rhyme suggestions grouped by syllable count, with definitions and part of speech for each result.

    →
  • 📝

    Notes

    Free-form note tiles for ideas, references and fragments that do not belong inside a specific song.

    →
  • 💾

    Backup and Restore

    Export a full backup of all your songs and settings. Restore any time on the same or a new device.
