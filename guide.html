Scales

The Scales page shows you fingering for every scale and arpeggio in all 12 keys, with interactive piano keyboards for both hands.

Choosing a Scale

Tap any scale type at the top — Major, Nat Min, Harm, Melodic, Maj Arp or Min Arp. Then tap a key from the grid to select your root note.

Scale Types

  • Major — standard major scale
  • Nat Min — natural minor scale
  • Harm — harmonic minor, raised 7th
  • Melodic — melodic minor, separate ascending and descending views
  • Maj Arp — major arpeggio over 2 octaves
  • Min Arp — minor arpeggio over 2 octaves

Relative & Parallel

Below the key selector, the Relative and Parallel buttons jump you to the related scale instantly. For example, from C Major, Relative takes you to A Natural Minor.

Key Up

The Key ↑+3 button shifts the current scale up 3 semitones. Press it repeatedly to cycle through keys. When a scale is playing in loop mode, the change waits until the end of the current loop before switching — no interruption.

Playback

  • Press ▶ to play the scale at the current tempo
  • Press the loop button to keep repeating
  • Choose Both, RH or LH to hear one or both hands
  • Tap the BPM number to type a specific tempo
  • Scroll Sync keeps both keyboards scrolled together

Tap any highlighted key on the keyboard to hear that individual note.

Chords

The Chords page shows you every chord in the current key on a scrollable piano keyboard, and lets you loop chord progressions.

Chord Display

The keyboard highlights the notes of each chord as you tap them. The chord name and Roman numeral are shown above.

Chord Progressions

Scroll down to see a library of common progressions — Pop, Blues, Jazz, Classical and more. Each has a play and loop button.

  • ▶ plays the progression once
  • ↻ loops it continuously — press again to play out the current loop then stop
  • Tap the BPM number to set your tempo

Key Changes While Looping

While a progression is looping, press the Key ↑+3, Relative or Parallel buttons to queue a key change. The change fires cleanly at the end of the current loop with no interruption.

The Relative button cycles between a major key and its relative minor — great for practising both together.

Pads

The Pads page is the most interactive part of the app — a performance playground with three modes: Chord, Drum and Melody.

Switching Modes

Tap the mode selector button at the top to switch between Chord, Drum and Melody pads.

8 or 12 Pads

The 8⊞ button next to the mode selector toggles between 8 and 12 pads in all three modes. 12 pads extends the range with additional notes and sounds.

Chord Pads

Eight or twelve chord pads laid out in the key you select. Hold multiple pads at once to combine chords.

  • Tap the key button to change key
  • Maj/Min toggles between major and minor chord qualities
  • Chord Pattern selects the chord progression playing in the background
  • ▶ starts and stops the chord loop
  • ↻ toggles looping — press while looping to play out the bar then stop
  • ♫ Arp switches to arpeggio mode — chords play as arpeggios
  • ↑ Key queues a key change at the next bar 1
  • ↺ Key resets to the original key

Drum Pads

Eight or twelve drum pads with synthesised drum sounds. Tap to trigger sounds, or use the drum pattern buttons to play an auto pattern.

  • Simple, Disco, Rock, Breakdown — auto drum patterns that switch cleanly on beat 1
  • Fill — triggers a drum fill on the next bar
  • ▶ starts and stops the drum loop
  • ⇄ Move — rearrange the pads into any order you like
  • 12 pads adds Rim, Cowbell, Shaker and Ride sounds

Melody Pads

Eight or twelve pads mapped to the notes of the current scale. Play melodies over the chord and drum loops running beneath.

  • Pads are colour-coded by note
  • Swipe between pads to slide between notes
  • Multiple fingers play multiple notes simultaneously
  • Maj/Min switches the scale quality — queued at beat 1 when loops are running
  • ♫ Arp plays notes as arpeggios instead of sustained chords
  • ↑ Key queues a key change at beat 1

Layering All Three Together

All three layers can run simultaneously. Start a drum pattern, add a chord loop, then play melody on top. When you add drums to a running chord loop, they snap to beat 1 automatically — perfectly in sync.

Press a new drum pattern button while playing to queue it — it switches cleanly on the next bar 1, never mid-bar.

Fingering Editor

The Fingering page shows you the recommended fingering for the current scale on both hands, and lets you customise it to suit your own technique.

Viewing Fingering

Select any scale from the scale selector grid at the bottom of the page. The keyboards at the top update to show fingering numbers on each highlighted key.

Editing Fingering

Two ways to edit:

  • Tap a key — cycles the finger number 1→2→3→4→5→1
  • Input boxes — type directly into the numbered boxes below each keyboard for precise entry

Both methods stay in sync — tap a key and the input box updates, type in a box and the keyboard updates.

Saving

Press Save to store your changes. They persist between sessions.
