Melody & Chord Pads

A performance playground with three interactive pad modes

The Pads page is the most interactive part of the app. Three modes — Chord, Drum and Melody — can all run simultaneously.

Switching Modes

Tap the mode selector button at the top to switch between Chord, Drum and Melody pads.

8 or 12 Pads

The 8⊞ button toggles between 8 and 12 pads across all three modes. 12 pads extends the range with additional notes and drum sounds.

Chord Pads

Eight or twelve chord pads in the current key. Hold multiple pads at once to combine chords.

  • Tap the key button to change key
  • Maj/Min toggles between major and minor chord qualities — queued at beat 1 when looping
  • Chord Pattern selects which progression plays in the background
  • ▶ starts and stops the chord loop
  • ↻ toggles looping — press while looping to play out the bar then stop cleanly
  • ♫ Arp switches to arpeggio mode
  • ↑ Key queues a key change at the next bar 1
  • ↺ Key resets back to the original key

Drum Pads

Eight or twelve synthesised drum pads. Tap to trigger sounds or use auto patterns.

  • Simple, Disco, Rock, Breakdown — patterns switch cleanly on beat 1
  • Fill — triggers a drum fill on the next bar
  • ▶ starts and stops the drum loop
  • ⇄ Move — rearrange pads into any order
  • 12 pads adds Rim, Cowbell, Shaker and Ride

Melody Pads

Eight or twelve pads mapped to the notes of the current scale.

  • Pads are colour-coded by scale degree
  • Multiple fingers play multiple notes simultaneously
  • Maj/Min switches the scale quality at beat 1
  • ♫ Arp plays notes as arpeggios instead of sustained
  • ↑ Key queues a key change at beat 1

Layering All Three Together

Start a drum pattern, add a chord loop, then play melody on top. When you add drums to a running chord loop they snap to beat 1 automatically — perfectly in sync.

Queue a new drum pattern while playing — it switches cleanly on the next bar 1, never mid-bar.
