Scales & Arpeggios

Playing scales in every key with correct fingering

The Scales page shows fingering for every scale and arpeggio in all 12 keys, with interactive piano keyboards for both hands.

Choosing a Scale

Tap any scale type at the top — Major, Nat Min, Harm, Melodic, Maj Arp or Min Arp. Then tap a key from the grid to select your root note.

Scale Types

  • Major — standard major scale
  • Nat Min — natural minor scale
  • Harm — harmonic minor, raised 7th
  • Melodic — melodic minor with separate ascending and descending views
  • Maj Arp — major arpeggio over 2 octaves
  • Min Arp — minor arpeggio over 2 octaves

Relative & Parallel

The Relative and Parallel buttons jump you to the related scale instantly. From C Major, Relative takes you to A Natural Minor. Parallel takes you to C Minor.

Key Up

The Key ↑+3 button shifts the scale up 3 semitones. When playing in loop mode, the change waits until the end of the current loop — no interruption.

Playback

  • Press ▶ to play the scale at the current tempo
  • Press the loop button to keep repeating
  • Choose Both, RH or LH to hear one or both hands
  • Tap the BPM number to type a specific tempo
  • Scroll Sync keeps both keyboards scrolled together

Tap any highlighted key on the keyboard to hear that individual note.
