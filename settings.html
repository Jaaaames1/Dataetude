Themes, Palettes & Settings

Customise the app to suit how you practise

Open settings by tapping the gear icon ⚙️ in the top right corner of any page.

Themes

Four themes to suit any lighting or personal preference:

  • Dark — the default dark theme, easy on the eyes
  • Midnight — deeper and richer dark theme, true blacks
  • Cream — warm off-white light theme
  • Light — clean bright white theme

Key Highlight Palettes

Six colour palettes control how the left and right hand scale notes are highlighted on the piano keyboards. Choose whichever combination feels most readable and natural to you.

Haptic Feedback

Toggles vibration when you tap keys. Useful tactile feedback during practice — turn it off if you find it distracting.

Show Note Names on Keys

Toggles the C D E F G A B labels below every piano key across the whole app. Turn off for a cleaner look once you know your way around the keyboard.

Dark Piano Keys

Switches the piano keyboard to a dark colour scheme — black and charcoal instead of the default white and cream. Purely a visual preference; the scale highlighting and fingering numbers are unaffected.

Bluetooth Delay

If you use Bluetooth headphones, there is typically a short delay between the keyboard visual flashing and the note sound reaching your ears. This setting shifts the keyboard flash to match, keeping the visuals in sync with what you hear.

Adjust in 10ms steps until the highlighted key lines up with the note you hear. Typical starting points by Bluetooth codec:

CodecSuggested starting point
SBC (most common)150ms
AAC / Samsung100ms
aptX80ms
aptX Low Latency40ms

Not sure which codec your headphones use? Start at 150ms and reduce until the visuals feel in sync. Leave at 0ms when using wired headphones or the phone speaker.

Keep Playing When Screen Locks

By default all playback stops when your screen turns off. Turn this on to keep the metronome or drum loops running with the screen locked — great for hands-free practice without needing to keep the screen awake.

Default Key

Sets which key the app opens in every time you launch it. Tap any of the 12 keys in the grid to set your preference.

Default Scale Type

Sets which scale type is selected on launch — Major, Natural Minor, Harmonic Minor, Melodic Minor, Major Arpeggio or Minor Arpeggio.

Default Pad Mode

Sets whether the Pads page opens on Chord, Drum or Melody mode by default.

Default Pad Count

Sets whether the app opens with 8 or 12 pads across all pad modes.

All defaults apply every time you launch the app — set them to match your usual practice routine and the app is ready to go the moment you open it.
