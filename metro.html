Metronome

Full-featured metronome with tap tempo and custom time signatures

A full-featured metronome with visual beat display, tap tempo, and multiple click sounds.

Basic Use

  • Press ▶ to start and stop
  • Drag the tempo slider or tap the BPM number to type a value directly
  • Use −10, −5, +5, +10 buttons for quick adjustments
  • Tap Tempo — tap the button repeatedly in time to set tempo by feel

Time Signatures

Choose from 2/4, 3/4, 4/4 or 6/8. Or tap Custom to enter any time signature you like — 5/4, 7/8, 11/8, anything. The beat dots at the top update to match.

No Accent

Turns off the beat 1 accent so every click sounds identical — useful for practising without a strong downbeat emphasis.

Keep Playing When Screen Locks

By default playback stops when your screen turns off. Turn on Keep playing when screen locks in Settings to keep the metronome running with the screen off — useful for hands-free practice.

Click Sounds

  • Sine — clean electronic click
  • Wood — wooden block sound
  • Rimshot — sharp rimshot crack
  • Loud — louder version for noisy environments

Tap Tempo is great for matching a song you're listening to — just tap along to the beat a few times and the BPM locks in.
