Chord Progressions

Looping and exploring chords in any key

The Chords page shows every chord in the current key on a scrollable piano keyboard, with a library of chord progressions to loop and practise over.

Chord Display

Tap any chord from the list to highlight its notes on the keyboard. The chord name and Roman numeral are shown above.

Chord Progressions

Scroll down to find a library of common progressions — Pop, Blues, Jazz, Classical and more.

  • ▶ plays the progression once through
  • ↻ loops it continuously — press again to play out the current bar then stop
  • Tap the BPM number to set your tempo

Key Changes While Looping

While a progression is looping, press Key ↑+3, Relative or Parallel to queue a key change. It fires cleanly at the end of the current loop with no interruption or glitch.

Relative & Parallel

  • Relative — jumps to the relative major or minor (same notes, different root)
  • Parallel — jumps to the parallel major or minor (same root, different notes)

The Relative button is great for practising a major key and its relative minor back to back in one smooth loop.
