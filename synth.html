Synth & Sound

Shape the sound of your keyboard and scale playback

The Tone panel controls the sound character of the piano keyboard and scale playback. Tap the wave icon (∿) in the top right corner of any page to open it.

Presets

Eight built-in presets to get you started quickly:

  • Piano — classic acoustic piano tone with natural decay
  • E.Piano — electric piano with gentle chorus and warmth
  • Cosmic — spacious, evolving pad sound with modulation
  • Synth — bright synthesiser lead tone
  • Organ — sustained organ with harmonics that hold
  • Flute — soft, breathy flute-like tone
  • Sax — reedy saxophone character
  • Harpsi — harpsichord with sharp attack and quick decay

Parameters

Every parameter can be adjusted manually after selecting a preset:

  • Wave — morphs from Triangle to Sawtooth. Left = soft and warm, Right = bright and rich with harmonics
  • Detune — adds a chorus effect by layering a second detuned voice. 0 = pure single voice, 1 = rich doubled sound
  • Modulation — controls vibrato depth combined with reverb. Higher values add more movement and space to the sound
  • Vibrato Rate — the speed of the vibrato LFO in Hz. Lower = slow gentle wobble, Higher = fast expressive vibrato
  • Attack — how quickly the note reaches full volume. Low = immediate punchy onset, High = note swells in gradually
  • Decay — how long the note sustains after the initial peak. Low = short staccato, High = notes ring on and overlap
  • Sustain — 0 = piano-style (volume drops off naturally), 1 = organ-style (note holds at full volume indefinitely while held)
  • Filter Q — resonance on the filter. 0 = warm and flat, higher values add a peaking, wailing quality
  • Stereo Width — spreads the sound across the stereo field. Bass notes pan left, treble notes pan right for a natural piano feel

Test Note

The Test button in the bottom right plays a C4 note so you can hear your changes in real time without leaving the panel.

Restore Defaults

Resets all parameters back to the default Piano preset.

For a realistic piano sound, try the Piano preset then reduce Stereo Width slightly if you prefer a more centred sound. Increase Decay for a more resonant, concert hall feel.
