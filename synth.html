Synth & Sound

Shape the sound of your keyboard and scale playback

The Synth panel controls the sound character of the piano keyboard and scale playback. Tap the wave icon (∿) in the top right corner of any page to open it.

Wave Type

Choose the core waveform that forms the basis of your sound:

  • Sine — pure, smooth tone. Closest to a clean piano or flute
  • Triangle — slightly brighter than sine, soft and warm
  • Sawtooth — rich and bright with lots of harmonics. Closest to strings or brass
  • Square — hollow and buzzy. Classic synth or clarinet character

Attack

Controls how quickly the note reaches full volume after you tap a key. Short attack = immediate, punchy sound. Long attack = the note swells in gradually, like bowing a string.

Decay

Controls how long the note rings after it reaches peak volume. Short decay = staccato, notes cut off quickly. Long decay = notes ring on and overlap, creating a fuller sound.

Harmonics

Adds upper overtones on top of the base waveform to thicken and brighten the sound. Low = clean and simple. High = bright, complex, organ-like.

Reverb

Adds room ambience to the sound. Low = dry and close, like a practice room. High = spacious and distant, like a concert hall.

Noise Mix

Blends a noise layer into the sound for a breathier, more organic texture — useful for simulating the breath or bow noise of acoustic instruments.

For a piano-like sound try Sine wave, short attack, medium decay, harmonics around 20%, and a small amount of reverb.
