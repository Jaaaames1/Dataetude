Pads Loop Lab

Themes & Settings

Customise the app to suit how you play

Open settings by tapping the gear icon in the top right corner of any page.

Themes

  • Midnight — deeper and richer dark theme, true blacks
  • Dark — the default dark theme, easy on the eyes
  • High Contrast — bold black and yellow, maximum readability
  • Light — clean bright white theme

Haptic Feedback

Toggles vibration on pad taps. Turn it off if you find it distracting.

Playback

Keep playing in background — when on, playback continues if the screen locks or you switch to another app.

Defaults

  • Default Pad Mode — which page opens on launch: Chord, Drum or Melody
  • Default Pad Count — 8 or 12 pads on launch

All defaults apply every time you launch the app — set them to match your usual setup and Pads Loop Lab is ready to go the moment you open it.
