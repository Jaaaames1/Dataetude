Sample Pads — record, import, loop

Melody Loops, Chords and Sample Pads all share one recording environment — the same 8 loop slots, the same Save/Library, even the same tone. Record a chord loop on the Chords page, switch to Melody Loops, and it's sitting right there ready to play alongside whatever you record next.

What's in the app
  • 🎵

    Melody Loops

    Chord-tone pads for improvising melodies over a progression, with independent Pad and Loop octaves and a shared loop recorder.

    →
  • 🎶

    Chords

    Every diatonic chord in the current key on 8 or 12 pads. Record your own chord loops, or play along to a built-in progression library.

    →
  • 🥁

    Drums

    Four drum patterns with queued fills, tempo and volume control, and a full 12-pad kit you can rearrange with Move mode.

    →
  • 🔊

    Sample Pads

    Record your own sounds, import audio files, or choose from 36 built-in sounds, then loop them alongside everything else.

    →
  • ⚙️

    Themes & Settings

    Four themes, haptic feedback, background playback and configurable defaults for how the app opens.
